A new hero, new abilities, and a proper end-of-run screen.

### What's new
- **4th character: the Necromancer.** Fires a fan of bone shards. Ability **Bone Effigy** raises a decoy that nearby enemies chase, then it bursts.
- **Hexblade Witch has a new ability: Hex Snare.** Hurl a hex ahead; enemies caught in it are bound in place and take +50% damage. (Blink is gone.)
- **New Run Over / Victory screen** with run totals and a card per player: kills, damage, boss damage, hearts lost, coins, XP, your build, and co-op awards.
- **Level-up cards** now show the upgrade's level (e.g. Lv 2 -> 3 of 5) and your stat before -> after.

### What to test
- Do all four abilities feel different and useful? Try a 4-player game with one of each.
- Are the hex circle and the effigy easy to see in a busy fight?
- Does the end-of-run screen show the right numbers?

Post bugs in **#bug-reports** (include the version from the bottom-right of the menu) and thoughts in **#feedback**.
