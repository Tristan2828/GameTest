Everything from our first co-op run, plus difficulty settings, custom games and a smoother boss fight.

### What's new
- **Fewer level-up screens.** About half as many level-ups, spread more evenly. Bigger teams need more XP per level (more enemies = more XP).
- **3, 2, 1 countdown** before the game resumes after a level-up or the shop.
- **Level-up cards:** your pick stays highlighted while others choose, and squares show how many times you've taken each upgrade.
- **Weapon icons** with level pips in the HUD, on the end screen and over altars.
- **Arrows at the screen edge** point to teammates who are off screen.
- **Hero select shows your real color** (it was always blue), plus markers for who picked which hero.
- **Return to character select** button on the Victory / Run Over screen.
- **New boss arrival sound.**
- **Difficulty page** in the lobby: sliders for enemy health, boss health, enemy count, XP, coins and bonus hearts, with Easy / Normal / Hard presets.
- **Custom Game page** in the lobby: play a single stage on any map, set the wave length, turn the boss off, start with extra level-ups or every weapon.
- **Compendium** on the title screen: info on every hero, weapon, upgrade, relic, pickup, enemy and boss.
- **Performance:** fixed the slowdown for 10-20 seconds after a boss spawns in co-op.
