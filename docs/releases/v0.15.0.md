### What's new
- **Easier to keep up again.** Level-ups come more often than in v0.14 (but not as often as before), and Sharpened Bolts and Quick Hands are stronger. Stage 1 should be beatable again.
- **Revive your friends.** At 0 hearts you go down in a circle instead of becoming a ghost. Stand in a downed teammate's circle for 4 seconds to bring them back with half their hearts (two of you: twice as fast). While you're down, your screen follows a teammate; Fire / Ability switches who you watch. New co-op relic: **Mourner's Bell** (revive twice as fast).
- **Find your cursor.** Settings has an in-game cursor: crosshair, ring or dot, four sizes, seven colors.
- **Better run summary.** A star marks who was best at each stat, your build shows as icons, and the new **Weapons** page shows how much damage (and DPS) each weapon did.
- **Records.** The title menu keeps your best runs for each hero, with a score.
- **Metal.** Custom Game has a Soundtrack option: Classic, Metal, Metal boss fights, or Shuffle.
