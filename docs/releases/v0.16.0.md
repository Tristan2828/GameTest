A reason to explore the whole map, quests, more weapons and pickups, more ways to build your hero, and a way to tell us what you think.

### What's new
- **Map events.** Follow the edge arrows (and the blinking minimap squares):
  - **Champion lair:** a crowned champion sleeps somewhere on every map. Wake it, beat it, and open its **chest** for an auto weapon.
  - **Ritual circle:** stand inside while the dead pour in. When the ring is full, everyone inside heals and a pile of XP drops.
  - **Grave Robber:** a thief runs off dropping coins. Catch it in 20 seconds for a shower of coins.
- **Quests.** In the shop, each player picks 1 of 3 quests for the next stage (free). Finish it for coins or a free relic. Your quest is shown under your weapons.
- **4 new auto weapons (7 in total):** Chain Lightning, Reaper's Scythe, Hellfire Trail, Bone Spears.
- **4 pickups** that rarely drop: Heart, Soul Magnet (pulls every XP gem to you), Holy Bomb (smites everything around you), Frost Hourglass (freezes enemies and their bullets for 4 seconds).
- **The host can pause for everyone.** Esc on the host pauses the whole game; closing the menu plays "3, 2, 1".
- **16 new upgrades (25 in total).** Look them up in the Compendium's Upgrades page.
  - **Critical hits** (Keen Edge): some bolts do double damage, with gold sparks.
  - **Corpse Blast:** enemies you kill burst and hurt the ones around them.
  - **Ricochet** bolts bounce to the next enemy; **Hunting Bolts** curve toward enemies.
  - **Giant Slayer** (more damage to bosses) and **Executioner** (more damage to wounded enemies).
  - Simple boosts: bolt range, bolt speed, ability power, longer safety after a hit.
  - **Risky picks:** Glass Cannon (+10 damage, but -1 max heart) and Reckless Haste (shoot faster, move slower).
  - **One per hero,** only offered to that hero: Afterimage (Wanderer), Hallowed Blast (Gravekeeper), Deep Hex (Witch), Ossuary (Necromancer).
- **Send feedback from the game.** Title menu or pause menu -> Send feedback. Write a message and attach a screenshot. It lands in **#feedback** with your version and run info attached.
- The Compendium has a new **Events** page (events and every quest).

### What to test
- Do you go looking for the events? Are the champions too tough, the ritual too dangerous, the thief catchable?
- Quests: is it clear what to do, and are the rewards worth it?
- The new weapons and pickups: useful, visible, too strong?
- Host pause: does everyone freeze, and does the "3, 2, 1" play for everyone?
- Do the new upgrades feel worth picking? Any that are too strong, too weak, or never picked?
- Corpse Blast + Ricochet with a big crowd: fun? Any slowdown in co-op?
- Send one message with **Send feedback** so we know it works.

Post bugs in **#bug-reports** (include the version from the bottom of the menu) and thoughts in **#feedback**, or now straight from the game.
