Your names in the game, a new party lobby, a bigger end-of-run screen, and a round of fixes.

### What's new
- **Your name.** Type it on the title menu. Friends see it on your lobby card, over your hero, on the arrows pointing at you and on the run summary.
- **Party lobby.** The lobby now shows the whole party: one card per player with their hero. Click your card (or **Choose Hero**) to pick a different hero.
- **Bigger run summary.** Animated headline, confetti or embers, the run's bosses as trophies, a team score, a crown for the MVP, and a new **Highlights** page.
- **Exit Game** button on the title menu.
- **Tougher stages 2 and 3.** Enemies and bosses get much more health in later stages, more enemies show up from the start, and late level-ups cost more. Stage 3 should now be a real final test.
- **Minimap icons.** Every kind of marker has its own little shape (you, teammates, downed teammates, boss, altars, champions, rituals, thieves, chests).
- Arrows at the screen edge now only point to map events and downed teammates.
- The "Compendium" is now called the **Game Guide**.

### Fixes
- Shots now hit an enemy that's standing right on top of you.
- The Weapons page of the run summary no longer runs off the screen (it scrolls), and you can always get back to Overview (Esc / B works too).
- Champion chests open when you walk onto them (they needed you dead center before).
- The host pause is gone: it could put the game out of sync. Esc no longer pauses online (solo still pauses).
- Solo: no more "3, 2, 1" after picking an upgrade or leaving the shop.

### What to test
- Do names show up correctly for everyone (lobby, over heroes, run summary)?
- Is the new lobby clear with 2-4 players, with mouse and gamepad?
- Does the run summary feel more exciting? What would you add?
- Are stages 2 and 3 a real challenge now? Too hard?
- Any chest that still won't open, or an enemy you still can't hit up close?
