Every hero now has their own main weapon.

### What's new
- **Wanderer: Bolt Gun.** Same as before: a fast stream of bolts.
- **Gravekeeper: Reaper's Scythe.** Hold fire to throw a scythe where you aim. It flies out and comes back to you, cutting everything on the way out and on the way back.
- **Hexblade Witch: Chain Lightning.** Lightning strikes the enemy closest to where you aim, then leaps to the next ones.
- **Necromancer: Bone Spears.** A row of bone spears bursts out of the ground along your aim after a short warning.
- **Each hero has their own upgrades.** 12 new level-up upgrades for the scythe, the lightning and the spears (Twin Scythes, Split Bolt, Splinters and more). The bolt upgrades (Extra Bolt, Ricochet, Hunting Bolts...) are now Wanderer-only. Sharpened Bolts is now **Sharpened Edge** and boosts any hero's weapon.
- Altars and champion chests now give Orbiting Skulls, Seeking Bolts, Holy Aura or Hellfire Trail (the scythe, lightning and spears belong to heroes now).
- The Bone Charm and Hunter's Eye relics only show up for the Wanderer.
- New attack sounds, and the Game Guide and run summary show each hero's weapon.

### What to test
- Try every hero. Is each weapon fun? Too strong or too weak?
- Gravekeeper: does the short-range scythe feel good on a slow, tough hero?
- Witch: does the lightning hit what you meant to hit?
- Necromancer: are the spears satisfying, or does the warning delay feel laggy?
- Does the Wanderer feel boring next to the others now?
- Online: do your attacks show up right away for you and for your friends?
