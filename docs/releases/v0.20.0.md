Heroes get names, a Watch button shows what each one does, and the floors are calmer.

### What's new
- **Heroes have names:** Kael the Wanderer, Mortimer the Gravekeeper, Morwen the Hexblade Witch and Vesper the Necromancer.
- **Watch a hero before you pick.** In Choose Hero, press **Watch** under a hero to see a short looping clip of their main weapon and their ability. Pick them straight from the popup.
- **Calmer floors.** Tombstones, bones, skull piles and other decorations are half the size and faded into the ground, so they don't look like monsters any more.

### What to test
- Do the hero names fit?
- Does the Watch clip explain each hero well? Anything missing or confusing?
- Are the floor decorations still distracting, or now too hard to see?
