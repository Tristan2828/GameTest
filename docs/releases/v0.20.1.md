A smoother 4-player game: fewer stutters online when the screen is packed.

### What's fixed
- **Less enemy stutter online in big fights.** With lots of enemies alive, the host's enemy updates were too big for one network packet and got dropped more often over the internet. They're now sent in smaller parts, so enemies should move smoothly on everyone's screen even when the screen is full.
- **Faster host.** Crowds of enemies and big bullet storms cost the host much less work (lowest frame rate in a 4-player test went from 86 to 134 fps). Weaker PCs should be able to host bigger fights.

### What to test
- Play with 3 or 4 people and let the enemy count get high (late stage, boss fight). Do enemies move smoothly, or do they jump or stutter?
- Does anyone's game slow down in the busiest moments? Say whose PC was hosting.
- Do enemies still spread out naturally around you, without stacking on top of each other?
