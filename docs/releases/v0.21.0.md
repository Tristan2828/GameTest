Know when your ability is back, and earn Embers every run to spend on small permanent boosts.

### What's new
- **Ability ready marker.** Playing Mortimer, Morwen or Vesper, a small bar over your head fills while your ability recharges. When it's ready a gold gem pulses there, and a ring and a soft chime play the moment it comes back. (Kael's Dash recharges too fast to need one.)
- **Embers and the Ember Shrine.** Every finished run earns Embers: mostly from bosses and victories, a little from kills. Spend them on the title menu's new **Ember Shrine** for small permanent boosts: crit chance, move speed, ability cooldown, coin luck, pickup radius and safe time after a hit. A fully upgraded hero is only a few percent stronger, and buying everything takes around 100 runs.
  - Refunds are free, and **My boosts: Off** lets you play a pure run.
  - In co-op everyone brings their own boosts. The host can turn them off for everyone in Custom Game (**Ember boosts**).

### What to test
- Do you notice the ability marker without looking at the corner of the screen? Is the chime annoying?
- Does the Embers pace feel rewarding, or too slow? Do the boosts feel small but noticeable?
- Co-op: do your friend's boosts work for them (no rubber-banding with Swiftness)?
