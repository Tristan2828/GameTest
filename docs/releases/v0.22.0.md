A big change: you only move now. Your weapons fire by themselves, like Vampire Survivors, and your job is to dodge.

### What's new
- **No more aiming or firing.** Move with WASD or the left stick; that's it. Each hero's main weapon picks its own target:
  - Kael's **Bolt Gun** shoots the closest enemy.
  - Mortimer's **Reaper's Scythe** flies the way you last moved, plus one behind you.
  - Morwen's **Chain Lightning** and Vesper's **Bone Spears** go after a random enemy near you.
- **An HP bar instead of hearts.** Kael 100, Morwen 70, Vesper 90, Mortimer 150. Enemies touching you drain a little every half second, and the more of them, the faster (don't get surrounded!). Bullets hit harder, then can't hit you again for half a second. Later stages hit harder.
- **Healing:** Heart pickups heal 30%, every level-up heals a little, rituals heal, and you start each stage at full HP. New upgrade **Ghoul Blood** and new relic **Bloodstone** regenerate HP.
- **No ability button.** Grave Blast, Hex Snare and Bone Effigy are now weapons anyone can find on altars and in chests, and they go off by themselves. Dash is gone.
- **Hero perks:** Kael moves faster, Mortimer slowly regenerates, Morwen crits more, Vesper's kills burst.
- The mouse cursor hides while you play.

### What to test
- Does it feel good to only move? Do you miss aiming?
- Is the HP bar fair: too easy, too hard? Is getting surrounded scary enough?
- Do the four heroes still feel different with perks instead of abilities?
- Are Grave Blast, Hex Snare and Bone Effigy worth picking up?
