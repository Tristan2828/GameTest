A balance update for the auto-fire version: the four heroes are much closer in strength, found weapons matter, and coins are worth choosing over.

### What's new
- **Sharpened Edge** is now +25% of your hero's own damage, for all your weapons (before, +4 was huge for Kael and tiny for Mortimer). Glass Cannon and Cursed Skull work the same way.
- **Morwen** goes for the boss half the time when one is near, and her lightning keeps striking a lone enemy instead of fizzling. Her boss fights are about twice as fast.
- **Auto weapons hit much harder** and grow stronger in stages 2 and 3. Holy Aura, Orbiting Skulls and Hellfire Trail are also bigger.
- **Corpse Blast** is a little weaker (10 damage per pick instead of 12).
- **Coins:** enemies drop fewer coins in later stages and relics cost a bit more, so you'll have to choose in the shop. Champions, Grave Robbers and bosses pay the same as before.
- **Power-ups** are rarer (about 6-9 per stage), Hearts the most common.
- **Rituals** give 1.5 levels of XP.
- **Co-op:** more weapon altars appear with more players, each near a different player.
- **Quests:** Slayer, Gold Digger and Arsenal ask for more; Untouchable is 45 seconds.
