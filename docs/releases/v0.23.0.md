The rest of the auto-fire redesign: you now build your hero like in Vampire Survivors, picking weapons and passives as you level up.

### What's new
- **Level-up cards offer weapons and passives.** Each card is a new weapon, a level for a weapon you carry (your hero's own weapon too), or a passive. You carry up to **6 weapons** (your hero's weapon is one) and **6 passives**, and each levels up to 5 (your hero's weapon to 6).
- **Your hero's weapon grows with levels:** Kael's Bolt Gun gets more bolts, pierce and bounces; Mortimer's scythes get more and bigger; Morwen's lightning jumps further and splits; Vesper's spear rows get longer and spray shards.
- **Evolutions.** Max out a weapon and carry its passive, then open a champion's chest or beat a boss: it evolves into something much stronger (8 to find; the Game Guide's new Evolutions tab lists what you need).
- **4 new weapons:** Storm Lantern, Bone Cleaver, Grave Spikes and Spirit Daggers (11 in total).
- **Card rerolls:** you start with 1; buy more in the shop.
- Under your HP bar: your weapons (with level pips) and your passives.
