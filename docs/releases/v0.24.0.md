The game has a name: **Medieval Survival**! This build makes the changes from our first 4-player run.

### What's new
- **Boss fights:** when the boss arrives the horde crumbles away and the boss brings its own escort. After that far fewer enemies come in, so you can actually reach the boss.
- **Ritual circles fill faster** the more of you stand in them (x1.5 with two, x2.5 with four).
- **Spirit Daggers** fly as little knives.
- **"YOU" marker:** during the 3, 2, 1 after a level-up, your hero gets a ring and an arrow so you can find yourself.
- **Level-up cards:** new, bigger icons; no more text spilling over the borders; **change your pick** while you wait for the others; the **left trigger** picks a card too (one hand on the controller is enough).
- **End-of-run screen:** four players fit side by side, it shows the difficulty and every setting, each stage's time, a big score per player, and a new **Stages** page with the numbers for every stage.
- **Title menu:** **Join Game** opens a small window to paste the invite (or press Paste).
- **Lobby:** cleaner party cards; the Difficulty page shows which preset you're on, and a **Custom** marker with what's different from Normal.

The exe is still called GameTest.exe for now, so the in-game updater keeps working.
