**The playtest build.** Update from the title screen with **Update now** (v0.25.0 or newer). On v0.24.0 or older, download the zip below by hand once (the GitHub page moved to a new name).

### What's new
- **Real sound effects:** seven sounds are recordings now instead of synthesized beeps: hitting enemies, Mortimer's scythe, Morwen's lightning, enemy shots, grabbing a power-up or new weapon, a revive, and menu clicks. Everything else sounds the same as before.
- **New icons** for Fleet Foot, Quick Hands, Iron Boots and Bloodstone.
- The shop shows a relic's big icon.

### What to test
- Do the new sounds fit? Is any of them too loud, too quiet or annoying after a whole run?
- Do the four new icons read as what they are?
- Everything from v0.24.0 to v0.25.1 is still unplayed: hero unlocks, the calmer boss fights, faster rituals together, the new hero sprites.
