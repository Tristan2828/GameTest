GameTest - playtest build
=========================

Thanks for helping test!

1. Before unzipping: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock"
   (bottom of the General tab) > OK. Then extract it.
   This stops Windows from warning about the game.

   Already extracted, or no Unblock box? Double-click GameTest.exe anyway.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then
   "Run anyway". It warns because the game is new and isn't signed by a big
   publisher, not because anything is wrong. You only have to do this once:
   later versions install themselves from the title screen ("Update now"),
   and those don't trigger the warning.

2. Paste the invite your host sent you (looks like 203.0.113.5:7777)
   into the "Paste invite" box, then click "Join Game".

3. In the lobby, pick a hero (Kael, Mortimer, Morwen or Vesper; press Watch
   to see what each one does) and press Ready. The host starts the run.

Controls (keyboard + mouse, or a gamepad):
  Move   WASD / left stick
  Aim    mouse / right stick
  Fire   left mouse / right trigger (pushing the right stick also fires)
  Ability  Space or right mouse / left trigger (each hero has their own)
  Menu   Esc / Start (Leave Game is in there)
  Host only, after a run ends: R / Select to return to the lobby

Can't connect? Check that the version in the bottom-right of the menu matches
your host's, then let them know. They may need to open a port on their router.

New versions: https://github.com/Tristan2828/GameTest/releases/latest
(they're also announced in the Discord #builds channel).
Found a bug? Post it in Discord #bug-reports with your version number,
or use "Send feedback" in the game.

Level-ups: everyone picks an upgrade card (click it, or use the gamepad and
press A).

A run is 3 stages. Between stages there's a shop: spend coins on relics
(click / A to buy), then press Ready. Glowing altars give automatic weapons;
walk onto one to grab it. If you go down, a teammate can revive you by
standing in your circle.

Every run earns Embers. Spend them on small permanent boosts at the
Ember Shrine on the title menu.
